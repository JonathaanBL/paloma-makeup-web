Questions

Do you come to me?

Yes. I bring everything, including a work light. I only need a chair and a table near a window.

How far ahead should I book?

For brides, three to six months. For event makeup, one or two weeks is usually enough.

How long does the makeup last?

With good skin prep and setting, ten to twelve hours. For brides I stay for the touch-up if you book it.

What if I have sensitive skin?

Tell me when you book. I ask about allergies and adapt the products; if you like, we test first.

Do you speak English?

Spanish is my first language. I speak basic English and understand it well, so we can plan in either one.

How do I book?

Message me on WhatsApp or Instagram with the service, date, time and place. I confirm availability the same day.
